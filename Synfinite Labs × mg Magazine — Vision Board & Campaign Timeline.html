The Rollout

Campaign timeline — three webinars, one per month, September–November

Each webinar runs the same cycle: 30 days of pre-promotion, a live host event, then 30 days of on-demand access with rolling lead delivery — one webinar hosted each month in September, October, and November, so a new cycle is always starting as the previous one winds down. Branded content publishes alongside the September and November webinars: an editorial interview, e-newsletter inclusion, and an AI-optimized landing page.
